# Jenson USA Sales & Operations Analysis using Advanced SQL

##  Project Overview

This project analyzes **Jenson USA’s sales, customer behavior, staff performance, inventory management, and store operations** using **advanced SQL queries**. By leveraging **window functions and analytical queries**, the project provides valuable insights into customer spending patterns, product demand, and overall business efficiency.

---

##  Objectives

* Understand **customer behavior** and purchasing trends.
* Evaluate **staff performance** based on sales activity.
* Optimize **inventory management** to avoid stockouts/overstocking.
* Analyze **store operations** for improved efficiency and profitability.
* Demonstrate the use of **SQL Window Functions** and **advanced queries** in real-world business analysis.

---

##  Key SQL Concepts Used

* **Window Functions** (ROW\_NUMBER, RANK, PARTITION BY, ORDER BY)
* **Aggregations** (SUM, AVG, COUNT, MAX, MIN)
* **Cumulative & Running Totals**
* **Subqueries & EXISTS**
* **Joins & Filtering**
* **Median Calculation in SQL**

---

##  Problem Statements & Solutions

1. Find the **total number of products sold** by each store.
2. Calculate the **cumulative sum of quantities sold** for each product over time.
3. Identify the **highest total sales product** in each category.
4. Find the **customer who spent the most money** on orders.
5. Retrieve the **highest-priced product** in each category.
6. Count the **total number of orders per customer per store**.
7. List **staff members who have not made any sales**.
8. Identify the **top 3 most sold products** by quantity.
9. Find the **median price** from the product list.
10. List products that have **never been ordered** (using EXISTS).
11. Find **staff members with above-average sales**.
12. Identify **customers who ordered from every product category**.

---

##  Insights Gained

* Determined **top-selling products** and categories.
* Identified **premium customers** with the highest spending.
* Detected **inactive staff members** and those outperforming peers.
* Highlighted **inventory optimization opportunities**.
* Measured **store performance** across different locations.
